Every guard he spares or kills decides who he becomes

Every guard has eyes and ears. Run and they hear you; stand in their sight and the “?” turns into “!” fast. Patience, timing and the wooden scaffolds of the jungle are your weapons.

Hide

Melt into the bushes, sneak low and quiet, dive out of a patrol's sight and swim the flooded passages under the rock.

Hang

Dangle beneath a walkway while a patrol steps over your hands, then climb up behind him, or pull him down into the dark.

Strike, or spare

Silent takedowns from behind and from above. Or put a guard to sleep with the Sleeper Hold or a dart, and hope no comrade wakes him.

Run the canopy

Leap the scaffold decks, climb lianas, swing across gaps and slide down ziplines onto the guards below.

The Reaper's Fang

“Every soul you take, the Fang keeps a piece of you.”

Raiders with muskets they never had before burned Kai's river village and dragged his sister Lira away in chains. Dying in the ashes, Mother Ayo gave him the tribe's forbidden relic. Behind the raids stands a foreign colonel, buying captives to dig for the Heart of the Jungle.

  • Throw the Fang: three throws in every level.
  • Fall with the Fang fed, and it gives you the moment back.
  • Light Fang stones along the way to hold your place.
  • What you feed it, or refuse it, leads to one of two endings.

See it in action

Painted worlds that move: wind in the canopy, fireflies, blizzards and spirit mist, with music and ambience for every land.

128 levels, eight worlds, nine bosses

Follow the chain of captives from the Emerald Jungle to the Spirit Realm. Five boss fights let you turn the arena against your enemy: rotten bridges, rolling boulders, quicksand, erupting vents and spirit portals.

World 1

Emerald Jungle

Scaffold towers, lookouts and the Red Thorn tribe's patrols. Boss: Chief Grukka.

World 2

Mangrove Swamp

Stilt villages, black water and crocodiles. Boss: the Bog Witch.

World 3

Lost Temple

Pillared halls, ziplines and stone guardians. Boss: the Stone Idol.

World 4

Misty Highlands

Hound handlers, and a wind that carries your steps. Boss: Warlord Taro.

World 5

Sunscorch Canyon

Mesas, quicksand pans and dust storms. Boss: the Scorpion King.

World 6

Ember Volcano

Lava, and steam vents that throw you high. Boss: the Fire Shaman.

World 7

Frozen Peaks

Ice that keeps no footprints, crevasses and blizzards. Boss: the Frost Giant.

World 8

Spirit Realm

Violet mist and floating isles. Bosses: Colonel Blackwood and the Reaper's Shadow.

Beyond the story

The camp

Spend the souls you collect on moves, skills, weapons and outfits. The camp grows as Kai frees people.

The Watchtower

A Daily Hunt: a level you beat, from the other side, at another time of day, with a bounty and a streak. Or the Endless Hunt: one life, hunt after hunt.

The Old Fort

Build your own levels from every world's pieces and guards, beat them, share them, and play the ones other hunters made.
