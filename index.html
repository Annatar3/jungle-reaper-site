A stealth assassin in a living jungle

Every guard has eyes and ears. Run and they hear you; stand in their sight and the “?” turns into “!” fast. Patience, timing and the wooden scaffolds of the jungle are your weapons.

🌿

Hide

Melt into the bushes, sneak low and quiet, dive into the swamp to vanish beneath the surface.

🪢

Hang

Dangle beneath a walkway while a patrol steps over your hands, then pull him down into the dark.

🗡️

Strike

Silent takedowns from behind, from above and from below. Take a whole squad, one by one.

🌴

Run the canopy

Leap the scaffolds, climb lianas, swing across chasms, wall-jump and roll out of the drop.

Hand-painted, alive

Wind in the canopy, birds and fireflies, sunsets and moonlit camps. Every scene is painted by hand.

World 2 · Mangrove Swamp
