Jungle Reaper — Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026. This policy covers the Android game Jungle Reaper (com.annatar3.junglereaper), published by Demch (contact: nikayroo@gmail.com).

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, souls, upgrades, outfits and settings) is saved in a file in the game's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device: the game has no internet access, no user accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send or share any personal or device data. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not ask for any permissions to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. (It may use your phone's vibration for feedback.)

Children

Jungle Reaper is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All data stays on your device. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Jungle Reaper → Storage → Clear storage). There is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data (for example ads or cloud saves), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
