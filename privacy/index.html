Jungle Reaper — Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026. This policy covers the Android game Jungle Reaper (com.annatar3.junglereaper), published by Demch (contact: nikayroo@gmail.com).

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, souls, upgrades, outfits, settings and the levels you build) is saved in a file in the game's private storage on your device. There are no user accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

Shared levels (the Old Fort)

The game goes online for one thing only: levels that players build and share in the Old Fort. When you open the shared levels or share one of yours, the game talks to our server (run on Cloudflare) over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

  • A level you share is public: other players can play, like and report it. A level is made of tiles, guards and a name picked from word lists. There is no typed text, no picture and no name of yours in it.
  • The game makes up a random id once, on your phone. It is not your advertising id and is not tied to you or your device. Our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, to know which levels your phone shared and to count each phone's play, like and report once.
  • Like every website, the server sees your internet address. It keeps only a hashed form of it, for one day, to stop anyone from flooding the fort with levels.

If you never open the Old Fort's shared levels, the game sends nothing.

What we collect

Only what is described above for shared levels. We do not sell or share it with anyone. The game contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not ask for any permissions to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. (It uses the internet for shared levels, and may use your phone's vibration for feedback.)

Reporting

Every shared level has a Report button. A reported level is hidden on the reporter's phone at once, together with the other levels by the same maker. It leaves the lists for everyone after three reports, and we review and remove it.

Children

Jungle Reaper is not directed at children under 13. Shared levels carry no personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a shared level in My Levels in the Old Fort and it is removed from our server. To have anything else removed, write to nikayroo@gmail.com. Your progress stays on your device: to delete it, uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Jungle Reaper → Storage → Clear storage).

Changes

If a future version handles anything else (for example ads or cloud saves), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
