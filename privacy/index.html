Jungle Reaper — Privacy policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026. This policy covers the Android game Jungle Reaper (com.annatar3.junglereaper), published by Demch (contact: nikayroo@gmail.com).

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, souls, upgrades, outfits, settings and the levels you build) is saved in a file in the game's private storage on your device. There are no user accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

Shared levels (the Old Fort)

When you open the shared levels or share one of yours, the game talks to our server (run on Cloudflare) over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

  • A level you share is public: other players can play, like and report it. A level is made of tiles, guards and a name picked from word lists. There is no typed text, no picture and no name of yours in it.
  • The game makes up a random id once, on your phone. It is not your advertising id and is not tied to you or your device. Our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, to know which levels your phone shared and to count each phone's play, like and report once.
  • Like every website, the server sees your internet address. It keeps only a hashed form of it, for one day, to stop anyone from flooding the fort with levels.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can watch one for a revive, double souls, free souls or to unlock an item. They come from Google AdMob. To show them, Google's ads software on your phone collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives a rough location), how you use the ads (launches, taps, views) and diagnostic data, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. In Europe and the UK the game asks for your consent first, and Settings → "Privacy choices" lets you change it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Anonymous play statistics

Jungle Reaper can send anonymous statistics about how the game is played, such as which level was started or finished, where a hunt ended, how long a session lasted, whether an ad was watched to the end, and which version of the game is installed. They are sent to our own server (Cloudflare, over an encrypted connection). They carry a random number the game makes once on your phone; it is not your advertising ID, your name, your email or your device's identity, and we cannot tell who you are from it. We use these statistics only to fix levels that are too hard and bugs, and to understand how many people come back. We do not sell or share them.

In Europe and the UK the game asks you first; elsewhere it is on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → "Share anonymous play statistics". When it is off, nothing is sent and nothing is kept on your phone. Statistics are kept for 90 days in detail and then only as daily totals, which carry no number.

Crash and freeze reports

If you let Android share diagnostics data, Google Play shows us anonymous reports when the game crashes or freezes. We do not receive them any other way.

What we collect

Only what is described above. We do not sell your data. The game does not ask for any permissions to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. (It uses the internet for shared levels, ads and statistics, and may use your phone's vibration for feedback.)

Reporting

Every shared level has a Report button. A reported level is hidden on the reporter's phone at once, together with the other levels by the same maker. It leaves the lists for everyone after three reports, and we review and remove it.

Children

Jungle Reaper is not directed at children under 13. Shared levels and play statistics carry no personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

How to delete what Jungle Reaper keeps about you:

  1. A level you shared: in the game, open the Old Fort, go to My Levels and tap Delete twice on that level. It is removed from our server at once, together with its plays, likes and reports.
  2. Your play statistics: turn off Settings → "Share anonymous play statistics", then write to nikayroo@gmail.com with the number shown under Settings → "My number". We delete the statistics tied to it within 30 days.
  3. Ads data: it is held by Google, not by us. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and see Google's page for the rest.
  4. Anything else: write to nikayroo@gmail.com with the codes of your shared levels (each card in My Levels shows its code). We delete those levels and everything linked to them within 30 days.
  5. Your progress and your unshared levels: they are only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Jungle Reaper → Storage → Clear storage).

What is kept: the hashed random id stays on the plays, likes and reports your phone gave to other players' levels, until those levels are deleted (it cannot be linked to you). The daily totals of the play statistics stay; they carry no number. The hashed internet address is deleted automatically after two days at most.

Changes

If a future version handles anything else (for example cloud saves), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
